Result 3 · robustness and tissue

It replicates in new donors and marks lesions in tissue

99% / 97% of discovery hits keep their sign in the validation donors. All shared genes keep their direction in both routes; 92–95% of route-specific genes stay larger in their own route.

On Visium sections, each route’s signature separates lesion from normal spots only on its own route’s sections. Four sections: descriptive.

VAL: 8 conventional, 6 serrated donors, 1 donor shared with DIS. Visium: OSF ST_master captures with pathologist spot annotation.

Third result: does it hold? The validation cohort is different donors. Of the discovery hits, 99 percent of conventional and 97 percent of serrated genes move the same way there, and the shared versus route-specific split holds too. In tissue, we scored the signatures on Visium sections that have both lesion and normal mucosa. The conventional signature marks lesion spots only on the two tubular adenoma sections, and the serrated signature only on the two serrated sections. With four sections this is a descriptive check, not a test.

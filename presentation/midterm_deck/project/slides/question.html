Background and question

Two routes lead from normal colon to polyp

Conventional

Tubular and tubulovillous adenomas. Usually APC loss and Wnt activation. Most of them stay benign.

Serrated

Sessile serrated lesions and hyperplastic polyps. Usually BRAF-mutant, often proximal colon.

Research question

Which epithelial programs separate a polyp from the same donor’s normal mucosa, how much of that program is shared by both routes versus route-specific, and does it hold across donors and in tissue?

Colorectal cancer starts from polyps, and there are two main precursor routes. Conventional adenomas usually start with APC loss and Wnt activation. Serrated lesions are usually BRAF-mutant and sit in the proximal colon. Earlier atlas work compared polyps to normal tissue across different people. We compare each polyp to the same person’s normal mucosa, and then ask how much of the change is common to both routes and how much belongs to one.

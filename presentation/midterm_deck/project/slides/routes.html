Second result. We sum each specimen’s cells into one profile and fit DESeq2 with donor in the model, separately per route. Plotting the two routes against each other splits the genes. 224 change the same way in both routes: mucin and REG4 go up, mature colonocyte genes go down. 53 are conventional-only, and they are classic Wnt targets, which fits APC-driven adenomas. 68 are serrated-only, led by gastric-type genes like TFF1 and TFF2, which fits the known gastric metaplasia of serrated lesions. These are known biology, which tells us the pipeline recovers real signal.

As the textbook says, each route adds its own new cell type

One line = one person. Our label-free map finds 9 in 10 of these new cells on its own.

Each line is one person, from their healthy lining to their polyp. The atlas found one new cell type per route, cells that only polyps have: adenoma-type cells in conventional polyps and serrated-type cells in serrated polyps. Person by person, adenoma-type cells rise in 16 of 19 people and serrated-type cells in 13 of 15. This matches the textbook picture from Chen and colleagues. And the new cells are not just a label: our own label-free map from the first slide finds about 9 in 10 of them as cells with no healthy look-alike. (DIS and VAL pooled; labels from Chen et al. 2021. Captured share: adenoma-type 88.5 / 91.0 percent, serrated-type 96.4 / 97.4 percent, DIS / VAL.)

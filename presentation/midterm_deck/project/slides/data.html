Sources: CELLxGENE Vanderbilt H5ADs (Chen et al. 2021, Cell); OSF hftq2 (Heiser et al. 2023, Cell). Paired = donor has normal mucosa and that polyp type.

We hold two donor-disjoint epithelial single-cell objects, about 123 thousand cells. Every cell carries its donor, specimen and pathology, and the raw counts are there, so the paired design is real. That gives 19 conventional and 15 serrated donors with their own normal tissue: we discover in DIS and validate in VAL. Two things were not as planned. The pre-segmented MxIF tables on OSF turned out to be empty files, so our spatial arm moved to Visium polyp sections that come with a pathologist’s spot annotation. And three larger objects on Synapse are still blocked.

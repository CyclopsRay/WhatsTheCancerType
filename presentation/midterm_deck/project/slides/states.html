Result 1 · cell-state mixture

Each route gains its own lesion state, donor by donor

Adenoma-specific cells rise in 16 of 19 conventional donors and serrated-specific cells in 13 of 15 serrated donors, both cohorts. Hyperplastic polyps carry serrated cells like SSLs, so pooling them as one route is supported.

Line = one donor, polyp vs that donor’s normal mucosa. Labels from Chen et al. 2021. Median rise: ASC +24 / +47 points, SSC +21 / +17 points (DIS / VAL).

First result. Using the published cell-state labels, we compare each polyp to the same donor’s normal mucosa. Each line is one donor. Adenoma-specific cells, ASC, appear in conventional polyps in 16 of 19 donors across both cohorts. Serrated-specific cells, SSC, appear in serrated lesions in 13 of 15. So polyps are not just a reshuffling of normal states: each route adds its own state. This partly answers the optimal-transport question from our roadmap with a standard analysis.
